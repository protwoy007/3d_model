How to take the photos
  1. Put the object on a plain surface. Walk around it. Do not spin the object, because the background must stay still.
  2. Take 20 to 60 photos in a full circle. Each photo should overlap the last by about two thirds.
  3. Make a second circle higher up, and a third lower down if you can.
  4. Keep the whole object in every photo, filling about half of the frame.
  5. Use steady, even light. No flash and no moving shadows.
  6. Shiny, see-through, and very dark objects do not work well.

Your phone becomes a guided camera. It shows which sides are still needed in red and which are done in green, and takes the photos for you while you walk around the object.

What you need
  1. The phone and this computer on the same Wi-Fi, or the computer connected to the phone's hotspot.
  2. When Windows asks, allow Python through the firewall on private networks.
  3. On the phone, accept the security warning once. The certificate is made on this computer.
  4. A phone with motion sensors, using Chrome or Safari. Allow the camera and motion access.
